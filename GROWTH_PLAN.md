# Bible App (David) — Growth & Monetization Plan

## Model (as built)
- **Free**: limited chat, mood→scripture, Bible browser — $0
- **Plus**: unlimited text chat with David — $9.99/mo
- **Pro**: + David's spoken voice (OpenAI TTS) — $12.99/mo

**Key differentiator:** David's warm, human spoken voice. Lead all marketing with it.

## Pre-launch checklist
- [ ] Stripe prices/links live for Plus + Pro (monthly + yearly)
- [ ] 7-day Pro free trial enabled
- [ ] Analytics installed (PostHog free tier) — track signup → paid
- [ ] App store listings (ASO) optimized
- [ ] 4 social accounts + link-in-bio set up

## Positioning
One-liner: "A Bible companion that actually talks with you. When life feels heavy,
David listens, prays with you, and points you to Scripture — in a real, human voice."

Target audiences:
- Christians facing anxiety / grief / loneliness
- Young believers 18–34 (TikTok/Reels native)
- Journalers / daily-devotional users

## Content engine (organic-first)
Core format — "David responds to real feelings" (15–30s):
1. On-screen text: a raw feeling
2. App: David's spoken reply + Scripture
3. End card: "Talk to David free →" + link

Cadence: 3–5 posts/week/platform (TikTok, IG Reels, YT Shorts, FB Reels). Cross-post same clip.
Other pillars: daily verse + reflection, comment replies/duets, testimonials.

## Paid ads (after organic proves a hook)
- Start $5–10/day boosting best organic clip
- Meta Advantage+ / TikTok Spark Ads → installs/trials
- Kill <1% CTR, scale winners

## Free growth levers
- ASO keywords: "Bible chat", "Christian AI", "daily devotional", "prayer companion"
- Reddit/FB Christian groups (value, not spam)
- Church / small-group leader outreach (free Pro codes)
- Referral loop: give a month, get a month

## 30-day sprint
- Week 1 — Foundation (Stripe/trial, analytics, one-liner, accounts)
- Week 2 — Content (film 10–15 clips, schedule 5/wk)
- Week 3 — Engage & measure (reply, find winning hook, testimonials)
- Week 4 — Amplify (boost top clip, referral, pitch 3 leaders)

## KPIs
Installs · Free→paid conversion % · cost per trial · D7 retention
